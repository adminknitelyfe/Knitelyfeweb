Safety & Trust

A safety company has to be worth trusting first.

We're asking people to share where they are and who they're meeting. That's a serious thing to ask for, and it sets a high bar for how we build.

You decide what's shared

Sharing is something you turn on for a reason and turn off when you're done. Knite Lyfe is designed around active plans, not around watching people continuously.

Verification without exposure

The design goal is that others can see you're verified without seeing your documents. Status is what gets shared — not the underlying paperwork.

Data minimisation by default

We're designing to collect what a feature genuinely requires and no more. Less collected is less to protect and less to lose.

Location that expires

Location information is tied to an active plan. It isn't intended to become a permanent record of where you've been.

No selling your data

Your location and identity information are not products. We do not sell personal data, and our business model does not depend on it.

Transparency about what we are

We'd rather tell you plainly what's built, what's planned and what's still an intention than imply more than exists today.

Important

What Knite Lyfe is not.

Knite Lyfe is a personal safety tool. It is not an emergency service and it cannot guarantee anyone's safety.

It is designed to supplement good judgment, trusted relationships and established emergency resources — never to replace them. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services. No application can prevent harm, and no technology should be relied on as your only precaution.

Verification does not mean safe.

A Knite ID is intended to help establish that a person is real, that certain identity attributes have been confirmed, and that they are not entirely anonymous. It does not establish character, intentions, future behaviour or criminal history, and a verified person can still be someone you should not meet. Verification adds context. It does not replace judgement.

Common questions

Straight answers.

Is Knite Lyfe tracking me all the time?
No. The product is built around plans you start and end. Sharing is intended to be tied to an active plan rather than running continuously in the background.
Who can see my location?
Only the trusted contacts you choose, while a plan is active, on the terms you set. You can change your contacts or stop sharing at any time.
What happens to my identity documents?
The design intent is that verification confirms your status without exposing your documents to other users. What other people see is that you're verified — not the underlying information.
Do you sell data to advertisers?
No. Selling personal or location data is not part of our business model and is not something we intend to do.
Is the app available now?
Not yet. Knite Lyfe is in development, with a first release planned for 2027. Joining the waitlist is the way to hear when it's ready.
What security certifications do you hold?
None yet, and we won't claim otherwise. We're a pre-release company, and we think stating that plainly matters more than implying protections we haven't independently verified. Our security commitments will be published alongside the product.
