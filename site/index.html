Every day, people build informal safety systems out of group chats and screenshots. Knite Lyfe turns that instinct into something that actually works.
The gap

You can verify a package. You can verify a payment. You can verify a ride.

You cannot verify the person you're about to meet. More of life now begins online and finishes in person — and that handoff is where the tools run out.

Unconfirmed identity

A profile is not a person. Photos and names are the easiest things in the world to fake.

Plans that live nowhere

The details sit in a group chat somebody has already scrolled past.

No one keeping time

"Text me when you get home" only works if someone remembers to notice you didn't.

Help that isn't ready

In the moment you need it, fumbling for a phone is the last thing you should be doing.

Knite Exchange

Share your Knite, not your number.

Meeting someone from a listing or an app usually starts by handing a stranger your phone number. Knite Exchange is being designed as the step in between — enough for both people to know who they're dealing with, without giving away how to reach you everywhere else.

Send

Share a secure Knite Link through the platform you're already talking on.

Scan

Show your Knite QR and let them scan it, in person.

Tap

Bring two compatible phones together. A planned capability, subject to device support.

What they see

  • Identity verified
  • Photo verified
  • 21+ verified
  • Member since 2026

What stays yours

  • Phone number Private
  • Home address Private
  • Full date of birth Private
  • Identity documents Private

Verification adds context. It doesn't replace judgement. Knowing someone is real is not the same as knowing they're safe, and we won't pretend otherwise.
