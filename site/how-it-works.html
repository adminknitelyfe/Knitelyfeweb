How it works

The plan you'd make anyway — just one that doesn't depend on memory.

Knite Lyfe sits quietly around an evening. You set it up in under a minute, then get on with your life.

1 · Set up your Knite ID

Verify once, and reuse it. A Knite ID is designed to confirm you're a real person in the age range you claim — so that later, when someone reasonably wants to know that, you can show it without handing over documents.

2 · Build your Knite network

Add the people you'd genuinely call at 1am — a roommate, a sibling, a parent, a close friend. You decide who sees what, and you can change it any time.

3 · Verify who you're meeting

Before a marketplace handoff or a first meeting, send a Knite or scan theirs. Both sides see the verification the other chose to share, and neither has to give out a phone number to get it.

4 · Set your plan

Where you're going, who you're meeting, roughly when you expect to be done. It takes about as long as sending a text, and your network stays in the loop on the terms you set while you're out — without you narrating your night to a group chat.

5 · Get home

You close out the plan and the people who care about you know you're back. If something doesn't go to plan, they aren't left guessing.

Designed around

Three things we refuse to get wrong.

You stay in control

Sharing is something you switch on for a reason and switch off when you're done. Knite Lyfe is not a tracker, and it is not built for anyone to watch you around the clock.

It has to be fast

Safety features that take six taps are safety features nobody uses. Getting help should be the simplest thing the app does.

It can't cry wolf

A tool that alerts your family over nothing gets muted — and then it's worthless when it matters. Getting this balance right is the hardest part of the work.
