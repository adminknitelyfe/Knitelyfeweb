Digital University ID

Your campus ID, in your pocket.

Participating institutions can issue students a digital university ID designed for supported mobile digital wallets — a natural extension of the campus identity students already carry.

It sits alongside the rest of the Knite Lyfe experience, so identity and safety live in the same place instead of two apps and a plastic card.

What we're not claiming

We haven't announced compatibility with any specific wallet platform, door-access system or campus card vendor. Where an institution needs that, it's a conversation — not a checkbox on a marketing page.

Independence is the point.

Knite Lyfe Campus is deliberately not a parental monitoring tool. Students choose their own trusted contacts, decide what gets shared and when, and can turn sharing off. An institution that provides the software does not get a window into where its students are. Building it any other way would mean students uninstall it in week one — and a safety tool nobody uses protects nobody.

Campus safety doesn't end at the edge of campus.
