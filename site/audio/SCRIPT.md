# Knite Lyfe — 3-Minute Story
### Recording script — founder narration

**Target runtime:** 3:00 · **Word count:** 431 · **Pace:** ~145 words/minute
Read at a conversational pace. Pauses are marked — take them, they're what makes it
sound like a person rather than a reading.

---

### [0:00 — 0:25] The hook

There's a sentence almost everybody has said.

*(pause)*

"Text me when you get home."

Nobody teaches you that one. You just say it. To a friend leaving your apartment.
To your daughter heading out. To a coworker walking to their car after a late shift.

It's such an ordinary thing that most people never stop to notice what it actually is.

---

### [0:25 — 1:00] The insight

It's a safety system.

An informal, improvised one — built out of group chats and screenshots and
remembering to check your phone. Someone screenshots a profile and sends it to
their friends. Someone shares a location and forgets to turn it off. Someone says
"call me in an hour," and then everybody gets on with their evening.

*(pause)*

And it mostly works. That's the thing. It mostly works — right up until the night
it doesn't, because the entire system runs on memory and attention.

---

### [1:00 — 1:40] The gap

I've spent time on both sides of this. I've worked the door at bars, watching people
leave with someone they'd met an hour earlier. I've taught young people about to go
off to college. And I kept noticing the same gap.

You can verify a package. You can verify a payment. You can verify a ride.

You cannot verify the person you're about to meet.

More and more of life starts online and finishes in person — a first date, a
marketplace pickup, a new friend, a place you've never been. And right at that
handoff, the tools run out.

---

### [1:40 — 2:20] The company

That's what Knite Lyfe is.

It's not a tracker. It's not for watching people. It's built around plans you start
and end — where you're going, who you're meeting, when you expect to be done — and a
small circle of people who actually get looped in. Then it gets out of your way.

*(pause)*

We chose a knight because a knight isn't a symbol of fear. It's a symbol of
preparation. Somebody who shows up ready so that everyone else can go live
their life.

---

### [2:20 — 3:00] The close

I'll be straight with you about where we are: early. We're a Delaware corporation
building toward a first release in 2027, starting with a small group of students
before anything goes public. There's no product to download today.

*(pause)*

But the problem is real, it's getting bigger, and nobody has solved it properly yet.

People already look out for each other. We're just building better tools to do it.

*(pause)*

Knite Lyfe. Safely. Connected.

---

## Recording notes

- **Room:** small, soft furnishings, no bathroom or kitchen. A closet with clothes
  in it is genuinely one of the best-sounding places in most homes.
- **Mic:** phone voice memo at arm's length is fine. A $50 USB mic is better.
  Wired earbuds with a mic beat your laptop's built-in mic.
- **Take your time.** Read it three times through before you record. Then record
  the whole thing twice and pick the better one — don't punch in line by line.
- **Edit:** Audacity (free). Noise reduction, then Normalize to −1 dB.
  Export as MP3, 128 kbps mono is plenty for voice.
- **Save as:** `knite-lyfe-story.mp3` in this folder.

If you'd rather not use your own voice, a professional read runs about $75–150 on
Fiverr or Voice123. But for a founder story, your voice is the better call.
