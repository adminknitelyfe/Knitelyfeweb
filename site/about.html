About

It started with a sentence everyone has said: "Text me when you get home."

Nobody was taught to say it. People just do — because looking out for each other is instinct. What's missing isn't the care. It's the tools.

The systems people build on their own.

Screenshot the profile, send it to the group chat. Share your location, then forget to turn it off. Tell a roommate the name of the bar. Ask someone to call in an hour. Promise to text when you're home, and hope they notice if you don't.

These improvised systems are everywhere, and they mostly work — until the night they don't, because they run entirely on memory and attention.

Knite Lyfe exists to turn that instinct into something reliable.

To reclaim safety and peace of mind for people in social and public settings.

The name

Why a knight.

A knight isn't a symbol of fear. It's a symbol of preparation — someone who shows up ready so that other people can go about their lives.

That's the role we want the technology to play. Not something that makes you anxious about the world, but something that quietly has your back so you can enjoy it.

Your personal knight, from day to Knite.

The Knite

Preparation, not paranoia.

He wears a suit because the threat model changed. The armour is still there — it just doesn't announce itself any more. That's the posture we want the product to have: ready when it's needed, invisible when it isn't, and never the reason you stayed home.

What we believe

Four things that shape every decision.

Safety first

When a design choice trades safety for convenience, safety wins. That's the whole point of the company.

Privacy matters

Your data, your choices. We protect people's information the way we'd want our own protected.

Built on trust

Trust is earned through transparency and reliability — including being honest about what doesn't exist yet.

Stronger together

Safety is a community effort. The product works because people already look out for each other.

Where we are

Honestly: early.

Knite Lyfe, Inc. is a Delaware corporation in active development. We are building toward a first release in 2027, beginning with a small closed beta before any public launch.

We'd rather tell you that plainly than dress up a plan as a product. If you want to follow the work — or be part of the early group that tests it — the waitlist is the place to start.
