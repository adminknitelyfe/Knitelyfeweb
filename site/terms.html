Important

What Knite Lyfe is not.

Knite Lyfe is a personal safety tool. It is not an emergency service and it cannot guarantee anyone's safety.

It is designed to supplement good judgment, trusted relationships and established emergency resources — never to replace them. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services. No application can prevent harm, and no technology should be relied on as your only precaution.

Verification does not mean safe.

A Knite ID is intended to help establish that a person is real, that certain identity attributes have been confirmed, and that they are not entirely anonymous. It does not establish character, intentions, future behaviour or criminal history, and a verified person can still be someone you should not meet. Verification adds context. It does not replace judgement.
