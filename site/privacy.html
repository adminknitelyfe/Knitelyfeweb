Privacy Policy

Short, because we collect very little.

This covers knitelyfe.com — the website you're reading. The Knite Lyfe app is not released yet. When it is, it will have its own policy covering identity verification and location, which are far more serious than anything described here.

Last updated 6 October 2026.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form.

If you don't submit a form, we don't collect anything about you that is tied to your identity. There is no account to create and nothing to opt out of.

When you join the waitlist or contact us

We store the fields you filled in:

  • Your name and email address — required, so we can reply and tell you when there's something to use.
  • What you're reaching out about, and your message — if you filled them in.
  • Institution, job title and role — only on the Knite Lyfe Campus form.

Collected automatically with that submission

  • Which page you submitted from — so we know what prompted you to get in touch.
  • Your country — country only. Not your city, region or coordinates.
  • A one-way hash of your IP address — scrambled with a secret value so it cannot be turned back into your IP. It exists to stop one source flooding the form with spam. We never store the address itself.
  • The date and time.
What we don't collect

No cookies. No tracking. No analytics.

No cookies

This site sets none. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

No analytics

No Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no third-party tracking of any kind. We don't know how many people visit or where they came from.

No advertising

No ad networks, no retargeting, no audience lists. Nothing here feeds an advertising profile.

No selling or sharing

We do not sell personal data and we do not pass it to anyone for their own purposes. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to partners.

Who else can see it

Two companies, and we should name both.

Cloudflare

Hosts this website and stores form submissions in a database in the United States. They process this on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. Like any host, they also handle the network traffic that reaches the site.

Google Fonts

The site loads its typefaces from Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads — on every page, whether or not you fill in a form. We'd rather that weren't the case, and we intend to serve the fonts ourselves instead. We're telling you because it's true today, not because it's ideal.

Your choices

How to see it, change it or have it deleted.

Email adminknitelyfe@gmail.com and ask. You can request:

  • A copy of everything we hold about you.
  • A correction to anything that's wrong.
  • Deletion — we'll remove your record entirely.

We'll action it within 30 days and won't ask you to justify the request. If you're in the UK, EU, California or anywhere else with statutory data rights, this is how you exercise them — but we'd do it anyway.

How long we keep it

Until the waitlist has served its purpose or you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. We are not going to pretend to a precise retention schedule we don't yet operate.

Children

This website is not directed at children, and the forms are intended for adults. If you believe a child has submitted their details, email us and we'll delete the record.

Honestly

What this policy isn't.

This describes a website with two forms, not a product handling identity documents and live location.

Knite Lyfe is early. When the app exists, it will involve government ID, selfies, trusted contacts and location sharing — and it will need a policy written to a much higher standard than this one, covering verification vendors, retention of biometric data and who can see where you are. We'd rather say that plainly than let a short policy imply the hard questions are already answered.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If you're on the waitlist when something significant changes, we'll email you.
