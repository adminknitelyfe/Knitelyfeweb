Features

Everything in one place, instead of five apps and a group chat.

Here's what Knite Lyfe is being built to do — and why each piece earns its place.

Where it starts

Knite ID and Knite Exchange

Identity is not one feature among twelve — it is the thing the rest is built on. Everything below assumes you already know who you are dealing with.

Knite ID

A verified identity you set up once and reuse. Designed to confirm you are real, that your photo matches, and that you are in the age range you claim — without handing over the documents behind any of it.

Knite Exchange

How that identity travels. Send a secure link, show a QR, or tap two compatible phones. You choose which signals go, and your phone number is not one of them unless you add it.

Everything else

Trusted contacts

What it does: A small circle of people who get looped in when you want them to be.

Why it matters: You already have these people. They just don't have a good way to help.

Location-based safety tools

What it does: Lets your people know where you are while a plan is active.

Why it matters: Sharing that switches on for a reason and off afterwards is very different from being tracked.

Meeting safety

What it does: Puts the details of a meetup somewhere useful instead of buried in a chat.

Why it matters: If anything needs following up, the information is already where it needs to be.

Safety check-ins

What it does: A simple "still good" at the times you choose.

Why it matters: It turns "text me when you get home" into something that doesn't rely on anyone remembering.

Smart notifications

What it does: Keeps your people informed without spamming them.

Why it matters: Alert fatigue kills safety tools. Restraint is a feature.

Group safety

What it does: Helps a group of friends keep track of each other on a night out.

Why it matters: Groups split up. That's usually when things get complicated.

Battery awareness

What it does: Flags when your phone is running low while a plan is active.

Why it matters: A dead phone is the most common way a safety plan quietly stops working.

Drink awareness

What it does: A personal, private way to keep track of your own night.

Why it matters: Awareness helps you make your own call about when to head home. Planned for a later release.

Safety resources

What it does: Puts relevant help and information within reach.

Why it matters: Nobody should be searching for a number in a moment that already feels bad.

AI-assisted safety

What it does: Helps your network recognise when something may need attention.

Why it matters: The goal is fewer false alarms and better timing. Planned for a later release.

Knite Lyfe is in development. Feature scope and naming may change before release, and items marked for a later release are not part of the first version.
